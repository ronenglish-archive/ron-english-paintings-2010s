Notes

Also known as Monotype Snoopy vs Simpsons Guernica, the title used by Allouche Gallery and Ethereal Auctioneers.

The work incorporates source material from Peanuts and The Simpsons, including Snoopy, Charlie Brown, the Simpson family, and Nelson Muntz.
