Notes

This work appears in exhibition photography for Ron English’s Status Factory at Opera Gallery and in later documentation of Status Factory – The Art of Ron English at the Frank M. Doyle Arts Pavilion.

This composition was later adapted by the artist as a related hand-embellished giclée print titled All-American Temper Tot HPM Embellished Giclee Print by Ron English- POPaganda.

Sprayed Paint Art Collection records the related print as a 2015 signed hand-embellished giclée with spray paint on fine art paper, not framed, measuring 18 × 12 inches and issued in an edition of 40. The product description states that the hand-painted multiple was created to celebrate the mural Ron English painted on the Houston Bowery Wall in New York City.
