  • Title: Slash 'n Friends

  • Medium: Oil, acrylic and collage on canvas
  • Dimensions: 52 × 52 inches
  • Work ID: RE-001005
  • Signature: No signature observed on front.

Exhibitions

Status Factory, Opera Gallery, New York, New York, US, September 12–October 29, 2010.

Literature

Ron English, Status Factory: The Art of Ron English (San Francisco: Last Gasp of San Francisco, 2014), p. 125. ISBN 978-0867197891.

Ron English, Original Grin: The Art of Ron English (Paris: Cernunnos, 2019), p. 324. ISBN 978-2374950938.

Notes

In Status Factory, this painting is misidentified as Slash R'nF'R and paired with the date and dimensions for that work.

This composition was used as the album cover for Slash’s 2010 solo album Slash.
