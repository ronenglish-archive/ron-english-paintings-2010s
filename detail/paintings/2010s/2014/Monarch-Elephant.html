Notes

Ron English created and photographed a reference sculpture as a model for this work.

This work is documented on Corey Helford Gallery’s artwork page for Monarch Elephant, under the exhibition Ron English NeoNature — We Are the New They.

This work appears in BFA’s event photograph for Ron English Evolutionary Alternatives, TriBeCa Grand Hotel, New York, November 19, 2014.

This composition was later adapted as a related aqueous pigment print.
