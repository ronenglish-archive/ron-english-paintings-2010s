Notes

Ron English assembled and photographed a reference set as a model for this work.

This composition was later used as the cover for The Rabbbits’ album The Rabbbits in Delusionville and in related album-release documentation.

This composition was later adapted by the artist as a related giclée print published by Corey Helford Gallery and as a limited-edition print in the Vinyl Chord: A Revolutionary Record Store series.
